My first team analysis: Villarreal CF's annual accounts for the 2024/25 season (closing 30/06/2025), compared with 23/24. I went through the balance sheet, the profit and loss account, the cash flow statement and the notes, and built my own spreadsheets around them. The page below is my one-page summary. These are things I learned, not rules.

## What it covers

- **The three statements:** the balance sheet is a photo of the club on one day, the P&L says whether it won or lost money that year (in accounting terms), and the cash flow says whether money really came in or went out.
- **Player amortisation:** signing a player means buying his rights. The cost is spread over the contract years as a book expense, separate from how the fee is actually paid.
- **Player sales:** the sale counts in the season it's signed, not when it's collected.
- **The academy:** on paper it almost always "loses", because its accounts don't include academy player sales or the savings from not signing.
- **LaLiga's squad cost limit (LCPD):** a permission to spend, not money in the bank.
- **Wage ratios:** three formulas that answer three different questions.

## Key takeaways

- **Profit ≠ cash.** Villarreal made about +€19M in 24/25 (before tax) while its cash fell by €30M. Instalments from player sales arrive later than the profit.
- **46% of the money invested in the squad is still pending amortisation**, with an average contract length of 2.2 years. That cost will show up in the next P&Ls no matter what.
- **A big selling year.** 13 players sold in 24/25 for €114.2M (net transfer result €94.7M), compared with 7 for €24.1M in 23/24.
- **Spending right at the limit.** LaLiga's squad cost limit for 24/25 was €135.9M and the club used €135.6M (99.8%). For 25/26 the limit is €173.1M.
- **Wages depend on how you measure them:** 120% of revenue without player sales, 60% counting the net transfer result, and 51% using LaLiga's "first squad relevance" ratio. None of them is wrong; you just have to say which one you use.
- **Letting a good academy player go is expensive:** Gerard Moreno left for €1.5M in 2015 and came back for €20M in 2018.

Next I want to learn financial fair play properly (LaLiga and UEFA) and look at other clubs like Barça, Sevilla and Valencia.
